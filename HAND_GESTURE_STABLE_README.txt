MAMA AI STABLE HAND GESTURES V7.1
CLASSIC FINGER CONTROL + SIMPLE PALM SWIPE

This update restores the finger-control behaviour that worked smoothly before
V7. The normal launcher again uses the classic index-dot preview and the proven
V6.1 point, click, drag, right-click, double-click and scroll recognition.

The palm gesture is simpler:
1. Show one open palm and hold it for about half a second.
2. Wait until the preview says ARMED.
3. Keep the same palm open.
4. Move it left for browser back or right for browser forward.

You do not need to lower the hand, hide it from the camera, or show the index
finger before swiping. After a swipe, pause briefly while keeping the palm open,
then move it again for another swipe.

Controls:
- Open palm held briefly: activate
- Open palm moved left: browser back
- Open palm moved right: browser forward
- Fist held briefly: deactivate
- Index finger: pointer
- Quick index pinch and release: left click
- Hold index pinch: drag; release: drop
- Middle pinch: right click
- Ring pinch: double click
- Two fingers: scroll
- Ctrl + Alt + G: emergency stop
- Q or Esc: close
